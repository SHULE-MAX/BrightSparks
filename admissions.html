Application Process

Six simple steps to join our school
  • 1
    Contact the school or visit to express interest and collect / download an application form.
  • 2
    Submit the completed form with the following documents:
    • 2 passport photos (child)
    • 1 passport photo & National ID copy (each parent/guardian)
    • Immunization card or birth certificate
  • 3
    Provide a sketch map showing your home area and parent/guardian contacts.
  • 4
    Child attends an entry assessment — play-based for Kindergarten; written for Primary classes.
  • 5
    Receive admission letter, pay the UGX 50,000/= registration fee, and confirm enrollment.
  • 6
    Attend orientation and receive the school uniform list, booklist, and welcome pack before term begins.
