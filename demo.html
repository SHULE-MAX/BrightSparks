Enrolment

Simple Admissions Process

We accept applications year-round for both Day and Boarding sections. The process is straightforward six steps from enquiry to first day of school.

View full admissions page →

How to Apply

  • 1
    Contact the school or visit in person to collect / download an application form.
  • 2
    Submit the completed form with 2 passport photos (child), parent ID copy, and immunization card / birth certificate.
  • 3
    Provide a sketch map of your home area and parent/guardian contact details.
  • 4
    Child attends an entry assessment — play-based for Kindergarten; written for Primary classes.
  • 5
    Receive admission letter, pay the UGX 50,000/= registration fee, and confirm enrolment.
  • 6
    Attend orientation and receive the uniform list, booklist, and welcome pack.
