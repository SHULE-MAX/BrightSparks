About Us

More than a School
A Launchpad for Life

We believe every child carries a spark of brilliance. Our mission is to fan that spark into a flame that lights the way to a fulfilling, purposeful life.

"We Shall Reach the Shore!"

Our motto is a daily promise to every child, every parent, every teacher: the journey may be long, but we will arrive together.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, affordable education that develops a child's academic, spiritual, and social potential — producing God-fearing, responsible citizens ready to contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be the leading center of excellence in foundational education in Uganda, recognized for nurturing disciplined, innovative, and spiritually grounded young leaders who impact their communities and the world.

About Us

Bright Sparks Junior School, nestled in Seguku-Katale along the Entebbe Road, is more than a school it is a community. We have championed the belief that every child deserves access to world-class education regardless of background. With dedicated teachers, modern facilities, and a nurturing environment, we prepare children not just to pass exams but to thrive in life.


We follow the Ugandan National Curriculum, enriched with practical skills including ICT, liquid soap making, cookery and baking. We complement classroom learning with sports, arts, global study trips, and character-building programs.

Why Choose Us

World-Class Facilities in the
Heart of Seguku

We have invested in infrastructure that inspires learning, nurtures talent, and ensures the safety and comfort of every child.

Modern Campus

A multi-storey building with spacious, well-ventilated classrooms and balconies overlooking a manicured compound.

Modern Classrooms

Bright, colorful, stimulating learning spaces with modern desks and rich display materials to inspire young minds.

Swimming Pool

A safe, supervised pool where children learn competitive swimming and enjoy recreational water activities.

Cookery & Baking Club

Pupils in chefs' whites and toques learn practical baking and cookery skills building confidence and entrepreneurship from an early age.

ICT Computer Lab

A fully equipped computer laboratory where pupils develop digital literacy, educational software, and foundational coding skills.

Library

A quiet, well-stocked library with a curated selection of books and reference materials that ignite a lifelong love for reading.

Sports & Athletics

Football pitches, athletics tracks, and open play areas supporting physical fitness, teamwork, and school sports competitions.

Music, Dance & Drama

A dedicated MDD program with a Talent & Modelling Club where young artists discover and perform before audiences each term.

Educational Tours

Annual local and international study trips from the Source of the Nile and Murchison Falls to Dubai broadening horizons beyond the classroom.

Admissions

Join the Bright Sparks Family

We welcome applications for both Day and Boarding sections from Kindergarten through Primary Seven. Admissions are ongoing throughout the year.

Application Process

Simple steps to join our school
  • 1
    Contact the school or visit to express interest and collect / download an application form.
  • 2
    Submit the form with: 2 passport photos (child), 1 passport photo & National ID copy (each parent/guardian), plus immunization card or birth certificate.
  • 3
    Provide a sketch map showing your home area and parent/guardian contacts.
  • 4
    Child attends an entry assessment (play-based for Kindergarten; written for Primary).
  • 5
    Receive admission letter, pay the 50,000/= registration fee, and confirm enrollment.
  • 6
    Attend orientation and receive school uniform list, booklist, and welcome pack before term begins.
